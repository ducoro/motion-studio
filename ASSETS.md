# Asset sources and reuse

The root MIT license covers Ducoro-authored animation and tooling code. The media and brand graphics listed below retain their own terms; their inclusion does not change those terms or grant trademark rights.

| Material | Location | Source and terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 oil paintings | `assets/backgrounds/` | CC0-1.0, as recorded per image in `sources.json`; Wikimedia Commons source and download links, authors, dimensions, and hashes included |
| Bach piano recording | `assets/music/` | Kimiko Ishizaka, Open Goldberg Variations, 2012; CC0-1.0 source record in `source.json` |
| Mechanical mouse click | `assets/sfx/` | Original procedural synthesis, source and parameters in `source.json`; generated by the MIT-licensed `scripts/prepare-click.ts` |
| Glass maps | `assets/glass/optics.json` | Original procedural generation by the MIT-licensed `scripts/prepare-glass.ts` |
| Ducoro desktop icon | `sources/brands/ducoro-app-icon.png`, `assets/ducoro-app-icon.png` | Ducoro brand asset, retained for this Ducoro film; replace with your own product icon when remixing |
| Agent and model marks | `sources/brands/`, `assets/*.svg` | Existing brand graphics with their owners’ rights and trademark status retained; original source notes preserved in each component |
| Demo film, banner, and review frames | `exports/`, `docs/banner.png`, `review/` | Rendered examples combining the above material |
| Reference motion data | `assets/reference-geometry.json` | Measurements for the motion study, taken from the reference linked in `provenance.json` |

The seven component inputs were copied from Ducoro’s original production checkout. The seven model glyphs in `sources/brands/glyph-paths.json` were resolved from that checkout’s glyph registry: xAI, Moonshot, Z.ai, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral, and MiniMax. The xAI glyph comes from LobeHub Icons (MIT); the other six model glyphs come from Simple Icons (CC0-1.0). Their upstream links and the full LobeHub notice are retained in [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The exact snapshot commit is recorded in `provenance.json`.

The local reference video and audio are kept under ignored `.local/reference/` for production study. Previewing, rendering, and verifying the distributed film use the included geometry and independent production media.

React, Remotion, and other installed dependencies use their own licenses. Remotion’s applicable terms are described in its [license documentation](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license).
